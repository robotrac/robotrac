# 🤖🚜 ROBOTRAC

### Smart Compact Tractors • Precision Agriculture • Autonomous Farm Machinery

**ROBOTRAC TRACTOR INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED** is building the next generation of smart farm machinery for Indian agriculture.

We engineer **compact, precision-track tractors, agricultural implements, and autonomy-ready machinery** designed for orchards, sugarcane, horticulture, narrow-row farming, and demanding agricultural environments.

**Made in India 🇮🇳 | MIDC Paithan, Maharashtra | Autonomy-Ready**

---

## 🌾 What We Do

Robotrac brings together **mechanical engineering, agricultural machinery, electronics, sensors, and automation** to build practical solutions for modern farming.

### 🚜 Smart Compact Tractors

Our RT Series is designed around precision track widths for different farming environments:

* **RT 120** — 2.5 ft track
* **RT 160** — 2.8 ft track
* **RT 180** — 3.0 ft track
* **RT 270** — 3.5 ft track
* **RT 270 EV** — Electric platform
* **RT Autonomous** — AI-enabled autonomous robotics platform

Our tractors are designed for applications including **orchards, sugarcane, horticulture, inter-row cultivation, and precision farming**.

---

## 🧠 Autonomy & Robotics

Robotrac is developing an **autonomy-ready architecture** that allows agricultural machines to evolve toward intelligent and autonomous operation.

Our technology direction includes:

* 📡 GPS-RTK precision positioning
* 👁️ AI vision systems
* 📡 LiDAR & obstacle detection
* ⚡ Drive-by-wire systems
* 🔌 CAN Bus integration
* 🛰️ Sensor pods
* 🗺️ Autonomous navigation
* 📱 Machine telemetry & mission planning

The Robotrac RT Autonomous platform combines technologies including **dual AI vision, GPS-RTK, LiDAR safety sensing, and drive-by-wire control**.

## 🇮🇳 Made in India

Robotrac operates its manufacturing and engineering facility at **MIDC Paithan, Maharashtra**.

Our approach focuses on domestic engineering, local sourcing, and development of agricultural machinery suited to Indian farming conditions. The company states a planned **70–80% domestic sourcing** target and an annual production capacity of **6,000 units**.

Traditional agricultural machinery is not always optimized for:
